# Product demonstration guide

This is a gated runbook for a future accepted demonstration, not a record of a demonstration completed on this Linux host.

## Prepare and stop safely

Use a fresh disposable repository and a dedicated test company/owner on a controlled development backend. Record exact backend/Android/Mac commits and builds, migration digests, registered repository/base head, reviewed validation profile/image IDs, assignment/reviewer IDs and explicit operation budget. Verify existing device trust, private network/origin/session policy and recent-auth requirements. Provision no customer data or production permission. Do not enable unsafe cloud/artifact sandboxes or public network exposure.

Confirm the signed engineering-ingress slice has passed before presenting the run as signed phone-directed engineering. Keep general executive objectives and engineering deliveries visibly distinct until their relationship has been explicitly implemented and accepted. Verify an emergency stop and cancel path before starting, and preserve all existing human work.

## Show the useful outcome

1. On Android select the test company and registered repository. Enter the exact normalizeLabel scenario and criteria. Show the immutable objective/delivery/operation IDs after persistence; do not display cookies, tokens, signatures or keys.
2. Review the bounded plan and assigned specialist scopes. Start only through the accepted owner activation path and required approvals. Show distinct Agent OS session/task identities, not a staged roster animation.
3. Show registered isolated worktree/base IDs and the finite signed operation receipts as work progresses. Show genuine failure/blocker states when applicable, not fabricated progress or fixed sleeps.
4. Show actual lint/typecheck/test/build reports and the exact candidate head. Open separate reviewer findings and demonstrate that a missing reviewer or failed check blocks readiness.
5. On Android request a revision or reject a candidate. Then run a fresh disposable variation that exercises the optional guarded local merge with exact approval and real recent authentication where required. Show that a changed head invalidates the old decision.
6. Return to canonical terminal status and evidence on the phone. Distinguish “candidate prepared,” “local merge completed,” “blocked,” “failed,” and “canceled.” Never imply a public push, deployment or hosted production service.

## Show human control and recovery

Perform separate disposable runs for a repeated submission, signed replay, phone reconnect/background/foreground, worker restart, Mac disconnect, validation failure, protected-path request, stale head, expired approval, revoked device, explicit cancel and emergency stop. Use the failure matrix to place faults before/after dispatch, poll, effect and acknowledgment. Preserve the worktree and unknown outcome until reconciled; show no later unauthorized operation or accepted result. Do not damage an existing repository to make a dramatic demo.

## Evidence package and decision

Retain a sanitized manifest containing build/source revisions, intent/draft/delivery/task/lease/session IDs, repository/base/candidate heads, actual signed request/result identifiers or permitted digests, profile/validation/review/approval IDs, audit IDs, fault timing, cancellation acknowledgments and final phone/API states. Keep raw authentication material, customer source, unrestricted model output and sensitive arguments out of publications.

A reviewer must independently inspect the assertions and receipts, including failures. If hardware/provider/network/approval evidence is absent, label the demonstration NOT_ACCEPTED. If all required real-device criteria pass, recommend review of that exact bounded scope; do not automatically merge, push, publish or deploy it.
